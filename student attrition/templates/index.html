🎯 About This System

The Student Retention Analytics Platform uses advanced Machine Learning Techniques to analyze comprehensive student data and predict dropout risk with high accuracy. Our system evaluates 25+ parameters across academic performance, financial status, engagement metrics, and personal circumstances to provide actionable insights.

📊 Weighted Factor Analysis

Key Assessment Factors:
• Attendance Rate (25% weight) - Most critical predictor
• Academic Test Scores (25% weight) - Performance indicators
• Historical Performance (20% weight) - Previous GPA and scores
• Failed Courses (15% weight) - Academic setbacks
• Financial Status (10% weight) - Payment and aid status
• Engagement Metrics (5% weight) - Participation and involvement

🎯 Intervention Categories

Students are assessed and categorized into three tiers based on their dropout risk:
• Tier 1 (Performing Well): Students excelling academically with strong support systems
• Tier 2 (Need Monitoring): Students showing early warning signs requiring proactive support
• Tier 3 (Require Support): Students in critical need of immediate intervention
